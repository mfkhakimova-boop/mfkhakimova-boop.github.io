My own product

Where2Go: a bot that solves the problem

“So, where are we going in the end?” It's the question that comes up almost every time my girlfriends and I plan to meet. So many places, and yet it feels like there's nowhere to go. That's how the bot was born, as one way to solve it. At its core is a simple survey: everyone in the chat marks what they're in the mood for that evening — cuisine, drinks, budget, vibe. The bot ranks places from a shared base and returns a top list that fits the whole group.

The first version ran on a simple weighted algorithm, and every venue had to be tagged by hand. Well, AI handles that perfectly. Just type /discover best raves in Berlin and a list of matching places is ready. Handily, the AI tags the options in the right format straight away, so a venue can go into the base and you never spend tokens searching for it again.

Either way, people have the final say. It's either a vibe or it's not.
